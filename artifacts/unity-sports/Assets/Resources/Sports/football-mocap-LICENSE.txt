Soccer passing motion is derived from Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab
Motion Capture Database, subject 10, capture 10_01 (soccer - kick ball), 120 Hz.

Source: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/10/10_01.amc
Skeleton: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/10/10.asf
Terms: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/

The source terms permit inclusion in commercially sold products but prohibit
reselling the motion data itself, including converted versions. This capture
is not CC0. The embedded retargeted clip is part of the Arena Sports game.
CMU's collection was supported by NSF Grant #0196217.

Conversion, trimming, directional retargeting, support-foot adjustment and
ball-contact synchronization were performed by this project's asset pipeline.
See football-mocap-provenance.json for source checksums and football-strike.json
for the selected source frames and the contact marker.

The separate Idle, Walk, Jog and Sprint clips remain derived from the CC0
Quaternius Universal Animation Library. Celebrate is locally authored.
