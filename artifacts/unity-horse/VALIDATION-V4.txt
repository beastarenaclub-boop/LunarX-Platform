LIMITS
This is an improved game asset, not GTA-level photorealism. The jockey body
uses a baked riding pose with a scanned head and follows the animated spine.
Crowd poses remain static, with three scan variants and distance detail levels.
The original stadium is procedural and is not a surveyed real-world replica.
