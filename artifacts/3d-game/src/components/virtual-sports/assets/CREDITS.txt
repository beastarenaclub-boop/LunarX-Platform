Virtual sports broadcast assets

athlete.glb: derived locally from CC0 Vitruvian / CharMorph body and head.
Vitruvian: Sean Buckley and Olaf Delgado-Friedrichs; CharMorph: Upliner.
Additional source credits: SirMaxim (skin LUT), Upliner (rig), Mindfront
(eyebrows), J Hill (eyes). Source: github.com/ibrews/VitruvianGodot / NOTICE.md.
Source files already licensed and cached by this workspace's horse asset pipeline.
Sports cut, mesh reduction and Idle / Run / Strike clips are authored locally.
No Mixamo animation clips are included. Reproduce with tools/sports-assets/prepare-athlete.py.

crowd.png: existing generated grandstand artwork from this workspace's horse
and bike environment pack. Not a photograph of an actual match or CC0 asset.
Original provenance: public/assets/bike-racing/environment/crowd-provenance.json.
No image editing was performed for this reuse. Architecture, vehicles, equipment,
court textures and broadcast camera choreography are application-authored.

football-player.glb: derived from Innerscene's CC0 MakeHuman soccer player
(Micah). Mesh reduction, skinning, kit material variants, football kick and
celebration authored in tools/sports-assets/prepare-football.py.
https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/soccer-player-standing-3d-person-team-sports-f55b1b4f
Source SHA-256: 773cfa73bbcf659d7044934aaeacf1c3c9a49ae2955a51f56de7ad61ad1b6ffb
Idle, Walk, Jog and Sprint are retargeted from Quaternius Universal Animation
Library Standard (CC0), downloaded from the author's OpenGameArt listing.
https://opengameart.org/content/universal-animation-library
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Neither asset is from FIFA or EA Sports. No brand, player likeness or EA asset
is represented as licensed by this project.

football-fans.png: generated using the built-in image_gen tool, 2026-10-15.
Not a photograph of an actual crowd. See football-fans-provenance.json for the
prompt and original path. Used as a 4 x 4 instance atlas without raster edits.
